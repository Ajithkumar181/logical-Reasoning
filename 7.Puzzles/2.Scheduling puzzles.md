## 🌱 Step 1: What is Scheduling?

Think of your **school timetable** 🏫

* Maths → 9 AM
* English → 10 AM
* Science → 11 AM

👉 That’s a *schedule*.

In puzzles, you’re given some rules:

* *Ajith’s class is after Maths.*
* *Science is not last.*
* *English is before Tamil.*

You must arrange them correctly.

---

## 🧩 Step 2: Types of Scheduling Puzzles

1. **Linear Arrangement (Time/Order)**

   * Example: 6 lectures are held one after another. Who comes first, who comes last?

2. **Day/Week Scheduling**

   * Example: Seven friends teach on 7 different days of the week. Who teaches when?

3. **Circular/Table Scheduling**

   * Example: 8 people sit around a table, who sits where, and who is next to whom?

---

## 🎯 Step 3: Simple Example

**Puzzle:**
Ajith, Bala, and Cathy give speeches at 1 PM, 2 PM, 3 PM.

* Ajith doesn’t speak first.
* Cathy speaks after Ajith.

👉 Who speaks at 1 PM, 2 PM, 3 PM?

**Solution (step like child):**

* Ajith not at 1 PM → Ajith at 2 or 3.
* Cathy after Ajith → If Ajith at 2, Cathy must be at 3.
* Then Bala left at 1.

✅ Answer: Bala → 1 PM, Ajith → 2 PM, Cathy → 3 PM.

---

## 🛠 Step 4: Method to Solve Any Scheduling Puzzle

1. **Make a table/chart.**

   * Rows = persons/things
   * Columns = slots (time/day/order)

2. **Read all clues carefully.**

   * Write *direct clues* first (e.g., "English is on Monday").

3. **Use indirect clues.**

   * Example: "Maths is after Science" → place them in order.

4. **Eliminate impossible options.**

   * Cross out wrong placements.

5. **Fill remaining blanks.**

   * Use logic step by step.
