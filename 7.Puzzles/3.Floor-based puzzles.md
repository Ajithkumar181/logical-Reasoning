## 🌆 1. What are Floor-based Puzzles?

Imagine an **apartment building** with several floors (say 1st floor, 2nd floor, 3rd floor, etc.).
Each floor is occupied by **one person**.
You’ll be given clues about:

* Who lives on which floor, or
* Relative positions (like above, below, not on top floor, etc.).

Your task is to **find the exact floor where each person lives**.

---

## 🧩 2. Key Words to Understand

* **Above** → Higher floor.
  Example: "A lives above B" → A is on a floor number greater than B.
* **Below** → Lower floor.
  Example: "C lives below D" → C is on a floor number smaller than D.
* **Top floor** → The highest floor (say 8th if there are 8 floors).
* **Bottom floor** → The 1st floor.
* **Immediate above** → Exactly one floor higher.
* **Immediate below** → Exactly one floor lower.

---

## 📝 3. How to Solve Step by Step

### Step 1: Draw the Structure

If 8 floors → Draw 8 boxes, number them **1 (bottom) to 8 (top)**.

```
8 |  
7 |  
6 |  
5 |  
4 |  
3 |  
2 |  
1 |  
```

### Step 2: Write Given Clues

Put all statements in short form. Example:

* A lives above C
* B does not live on top
* D lives immediately below A

### Step 3: Place the Information

* Start with **direct clues** (like “A on 5th floor”).
* Then use **relative clues** (“B lives above A” → place B somewhere above A).
* Cross-check with remaining clues.

### Step 4: Eliminate Wrong Options

* Use “not” clues (“C does not live on top floor” → remove that possibility).
* Keep filling until each floor has exactly one person.

---

## 🎯 4. Example Puzzle

**Puzzle**:
8 people (A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H) live in an 8-floor building (1 = bottom, 8 = top).
Clues:

1. A lives on an even-numbered floor.
2. C lives immediately above A.
3. E does not live on top.
4. G lives below F but not on the bottom.
5. H lives immediately below F.
6. B lives above D.

---

### 🛠 Step-by-Step Solution

Draw floors 1–8.

1. Clue (2): "C immediately above A" → A & C are together (pair).
   Example: If A=2, then C=3.

2. Clue (5): "H immediately below F" → H & F are also a pair.

3. Clue (1): "A on even floor" → A can be 2,4,6.

4. Clue (3): "E not on top" → E can’t be on 8.

5. Clue (4): "G below F, not bottom" → G somewhere under F but not 1.

6. Clue (6): "B above D" → B must be higher than D.

Now try placements → After arranging step by step, you’ll get the final floor mapping.

---

## 🧠 5. Tips to Master

* Always **start with definite clues** (direct placement).
* Group pairs like “immediate above/below.”
* Use elimination for “not” type clues.
* Practice small (4 floors) → medium (6 floors) → big (8–10 floors).
