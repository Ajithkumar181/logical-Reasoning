# 🎯 Step 1: What are Age Puzzles?

They are small word problems where you find **ages of people** (father, son, brother, sister, etc.) based on conditions like:

* Present age
* Past age
* Future age
* Ratio of ages

👉 Mostly solved using **simple equations**.

---

# 🎯 Step 2: Golden Rules to Remember

1. **Translate words into equations**.

   * "After 5 years" → add 5
   * "5 years ago" → subtract 5
   * "Twice the age" → multiply by 2
   * "Half the age" → divide by 2

2. **Always assume present ages** as variables (x, y).

   * Example: Father’s age = x, Son’s age = y.

3. **Form equations** from given conditions and solve.

---

# 🎯 Step 3: Simple Example

👉 **Puzzle:**
The present age of a father is 3 times his son’s age. After 5 years, father’s age will be twice the son’s age. Find their present ages.

---

👶 **Solution like a story:**

1. Let son’s present age = **x**
   So, father’s present age = **3x**

2. After 5 years:

   * Son = x + 5
   * Father = 3x + 5

3. Condition: After 5 years, father’s age will be **twice son’s age**
   → 3x + 5 = 2(x + 5)

4. Solve:
   3x + 5 = 2x + 10
   3x - 2x = 10 - 5
   x = 5

So,

* Son = 5 years
* Father = 15 years ✅

---

# 🎯 Step 4: Typical Question Patterns

1. **Present → Future**
   "After 10 years…"

2. **Present → Past**
   "5 years ago…"

3. **Ratio form**
   "Father is 4 times son now, after 20 years ratio will be 2:1"

4. **Sum or difference of ages**
   "Sum of father and son is 50…"

## 🔹 Problem 1 (Easy)

**Question:**
A mother is 3 times older than her son. After 15 years, the mother will be twice the son’s age. Find the present ages.

**Solution:**

1. Let son’s age = (S), mother’s age = (M)
2. From “mother is 3 times older”:
   [
   M = 3S
   ]
3. After 15 years:
   [
   M + 15 = 2(S + 15)
   ]
4. Substitute (M = 3S):
   [
   3S + 15 = 2(S + 15) \implies 3S + 15 = 2S + 30 \implies S = 15
   ]
5. Mother’s age: (M = 3 \times 15 = 45)

**Answer:** Son = 15, Mother = 45

---

## 🔹 Problem 2 (Medium)

**Question:**
Present ages of A and B are in ratio 5 : 3. After 6 years, A’s age will be 26. Find their present ages.

**Solution:**

1. Let A’s age = (5x), B’s age = (3x)
2. After 6 years, A = 26 → (5x + 6 = 26 \implies 5x = 20 \implies x = 4)
3. B’s age = (3x = 12)

**Answer:** A = 20, B = 12

## 🔹 Problem 3 (Medium)

**Question:**
Sum of father and son = 60. 5 years ago, father was 7 times son’s age. Find present ages.

**Solution:**

1. Let father’s age = (F), son’s age = (S)
2. Sum: (F + S = 60 \implies F = 60 - S)
3. 5 years ago: (F - 5 = 7(S - 5))
4. Substitute (F = 60 - S):
   [
   60 - S - 5 = 7(S - 5) \implies 55 - S = 7S - 35 \implies 55 + 35 = 8S \implies 90 = 8S \implies S = 11.25
   ]
5. Father’s age: (F = 60 - 11.25 = 48.75)

**Answer:** Son ≈ 11.25, Father ≈ 48.75

---

## 🔹 Problem 4 (Placement-level)

**Question:**
Ratio of brother:sister = 4:3. After 6 years, ratio = 9:7. Find present ages.

**Solution:**

1. Let brother = (4x), sister = (3x)
2. After 6 years:
   [
   \frac{4x + 6}{3x + 6} = \frac{9}{7}
   ]
3. Cross multiply:
   [
   7(4x + 6) = 9(3x + 6) \implies 28x + 42 = 27x + 54 \implies x = 12
   ]
4. Present ages: Brother = (4x = 48), Sister = (3x = 36)

**Answer:** Brother = 48, Sister = 36

## 🔹 Problem 5 (Tricky Placement-level)

**Question:**
10 years ago, A’s age was 12× son’s age. 10 years hence, A’s age will be 2× son’s age. Find present ages.

**Solution:**

1. Let present ages: Father = (F), Son = (S)
2. 10 years ago: (F - 10 = 12(S - 10) \implies F - 10 = 12S - 120 \implies F = 12S - 110)
3. 10 years hence: (F + 10 = 2(S + 10) \implies F + 10 = 2S + 20 \implies F = 2S + 10)
4. Equate: (12S - 110 = 2S + 10 \implies 10S = 120 \implies S = 12)
5. Father’s age: (F = 2S + 10 = 2×12 + 10 = 34)

**Answer:** Son = 12, Father = 34
